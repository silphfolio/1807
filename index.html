How to use this page
Binder format
Pick the binder page you are filling. On a 9 pocket page the stitching sits off centre, so the left and right pages are mirror images of each other. Choose the one matching the side you are laying out.
1×1 Inserts Required
This is the number of 1x1 inserts required to fill the binder page completely given the chosen combination of art inserts and number of cards.
Lock
Tap on the lock icon on the dropdown menu to lock the value. That value will not be randomised with "Inspire Me".
Default
Return to the recommended settings for the binder format you have chosen.
Inspire Me
Shuffles the results at random, so you see a completely new spread of the Layouts.

How to use this page
Add images
Click the dotted box to choose files, or drag images onto it. They stay in your browser and are never uploaded anywhere.
Place an image
Tap a pocket to select it, then press “Add Image” to choose a file straight into it. If you have already added images, a row of them appears underneath so you can tap one to reuse it. You can also tap an image in the tray and then tap a pocket, or drag one across on a computer.
Adjust Crop
Reposition the image inside any pocket. Tap the button again to save your edits. Only works with the Crop to Fill option.
Fit dropdown
Sets that same choice for every image on the page at once.
Selecting pockets
Tap a pocket to select it, tap again to deselect. The buttons under the page show what you can do with the current selection.
Move an image
Drag one pocket onto another to move the images between them. Sizes do not have to match, and the pockets keep their own card or art designation.
Merge
Select two or four inserts sitting side by side, then press Merge to join them into a single 1×2, 2×1 or 2×2. Nothing can merge across the stitch line.
Unmerge
Select a large art insert and press Unmerge to break it back into single pockets.
Lock a pocket
Select one or more pockets and press Lock. A padlock appears on them, and they are pinned in place. Shuffle then only moves to layouts that still contain that exact pocket, so it and whatever is in it stay exactly where they are while everything around them changes. Locked pockets also cannot be overwritten, swapped or emptied, and they survive “Reset Layout”. You can lock an empty pocket too, to hold its position while you fill the rest. Tap the padlock again to unlock.
Shuffle
Moves to another layout with the same number of cards and inserts, and moves your images between pockets of the same size. Pockets of one size still get reshuffled even when the layout itself cannot change. Locked pockets stay where they are. Tap “Inspire Me” for a completely different layout.
Reset Layout
Empties every unlocked pocket at once, keeping your images in the tray.
Undo
Reverses the last change you made up to the last twenty steps.
Mirror
Flips the layout left to right. On a 9 pocket page the mirror belongs to the opposite page, so the binder format switches with it.
Favourite
Keeps the current layout in the Favourites row on the right. Every layout you open from Browse Layouts also lands in Recent automatically. Click any of them to load it back.
Download Layout
Saves the layout as an image.
Print Inserts
Download a ready-to-print file in A4 or US Letter canvas. All images are set to a resolution of 300 DPI.
Insert sizes and image resolution
Card / 1×1
A 1×1 art insert is 68 × 94 mm. Upload at least 804 × 1111 px for a clean 300 dpi print.
Two across (1x2)
One insert spanning two pockets side by side is 140 × 94 mm. Upload at least 1654 × 1111 px.
Two down (2x1)
A pocket pair stacked vertically crosses the row stitching, two separate pieces of 68 × 94 mm. Upload at least 804 × 2221 px and the Studio splits it for you.
Two by two (2x2)
Two pieces of 140 × 94 mm. Upload at least 1654 × 2221 px.
